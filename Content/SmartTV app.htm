About SmartTV App

SmartTV is a Premium TV streaming application that lets you discover, manage, and track your favorite television programs. With SmartTV, you can:

  1. Browse and search for thousands of TV programs

  2. Manage your favorite programs in one place

  3. Schedule programs to watch later

  4. Set reminders for upcoming episodes

  5. View your personalized schedule

  6. Customize notifications and preferences

Getting Started

To begin using SmartTV, install the application on your device and create or sign in to your account. Once logged in, you'll see your personalized home dashboard with your tracked channels, favorites, and upcoming schedule.

Key Features

SmartTV includes the following main features:

  • Dashboard:Your personalized home screen with quick access to all features

  • Finding Programs:Browse and search for programs by category or title

  • Favorites: Save programs you don't want to miss

  • Schedule:View and manage your upcoming programs

  • Reminders:Set alerts for programs you want to watch

  • Settings:Customize your account and app preferences
