Scheduling Your Programs

Schedule programs you want to watch to create your personal TV schedule. SmartTV tracks all your scheduled programs across channels you follow.

Schedule Navigation

  1. Open the SmartTV App.

  2. Select Schedule from the sidebar.

  3. You'll see your Program Schedule view.

View Your Schedule

The schedule shows:

- Total shows scheduled across tracked channels

- Calendar view with dates (Today, Tomorrow, upcoming dates)

- Category filters (All Genres, Comedy, Drama, Action, News, etc.)

- Programs organized by date and time

Schedule a Program

  1. Find a program in the Browse or Search view.

  2. Select the program to view details.

  3. Select Schedule or Add to Schedule.

  4. Confirm the scheduled time and date.

  5. The program will be added to your schedule.

Calendar View

Use the calendar to organize your viewing:

  1. Select a specific date to view programs for that day.

  2. Programs display with air time and channel.

  3. Sort by All Genres or filter by specific categories.

Program Details in Schedule

Each scheduled program shows:

- Program title and thumbnail

- Category (Drama, Documentary, Sci-Fi, etc.)

- Episode information

- Air time (specific time and duration)

- Channel number and name

- Status (upcoming, airing, completed)

Track Your Channels

SmartTV tracks channels you follow:

- View the total number of channels tracked.

- Programs from tracked channels appear in your schedule.

- Add or remove channels from your tracked list in Settings.

Manage Scheduled Programs

To view or edit scheduled programs:

  1. Open Schedule.

  2. Find the program in your schedule.

  3. Select it to view details.

  4. Remove it from schedule if desired.

Sync Across Devices

Your schedule syncs across all devices:

  1. Schedule a program on one device.

  2. It will appear on your other devices.

  3. All changes sync in real time.

Next Steps

Once you schedule programs:

  • Set reminders to be notified before programs air

  • Add programs to Favorites for easy access

  • Manage your schedule in the calendar view
