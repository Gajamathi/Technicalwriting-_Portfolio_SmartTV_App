Settings and Preferences

Customize your SmartTV experience by managing account settings, notifications, privacy, and device preferences.

Access Settings

Settings Options

Account Settings

View and edit your account information:
Profile Name - Your account display name (Gaja)
Email Address - Account email (gaja@example.com)
Account Status - Shows if your account is Active
Edit Profile - Modify your profile information
Change Password - Update your account password
Password Last Changed - Shows when password was last updated

Notification Preferences

Control how you receive notifications:
- Program Reminders - Enable/disable alerts for scheduled programs
- Reminder Timing - Set how many minutes before a program to notify you
- New Favorite Programs - Notify when new episodes of favorites are available
- SmartTV Updates - Receive occasional news and feature updates

Privacy and Data

Manage your privacy settings:
- Clear Browsing History - Remove your search and browsing history
- Personalization - Control whether SmartTV personalizes content
- Data Collection - Manage what data SmartTV collects
- Privacy Policy - View SmartTV's privacy policy
- Terms of Service - View terms and conditions

Display Settings

Customize the app appearance:
- Theme - Choose Light or Dark mode
- Text Size - Adjust text size (Small, Medium, Large)
- Language - Select your preferred language

Device Settings

Manage connected devices:
- List of Connected Devices - View all devices linked to your account
- Manage Devices - Remove or edit device names
- Device Information - Shows device type and connection status

About

Find app and account information:
- App Version - Current SmartTV version installed
- Build Number - Technical build information
- Feedback - Send feedback to SmartTV support
- Help & Support - Access help documentation

Account Actions

- Danger Zone - Account deletion options
- Delete Account - Permanently remove your account and data
- Sign Out - Log out of your account on this device

Change Settings

To change any setting:

  1. Select the setting option.

  2. Modify the preference or toggle on/off.

  3. Changes are saved automatically.

  4. If prompted, select Save or Confirm.

Reset to Defaults

To reset settings to default:

  1. Look for a Reset option in the specific settings category.

  2. Confirm the reset action.

  3. All settings in that category will return to defaults.

Account Verification

Some settings may require password verification:

  1. Select Edit or Change Password.

  2. Enter your current password.

  3. Proceed with changes.

Next Steps

After configuring settings:

  • Your preferences sync across all your devices

  • Notifications will follow your notification settings

  • Your account information is secure and private
