Troubleshooting

Login Issues

If you're having trouble signing in to SmartTV, follow these steps to resolve common login problems. Can't Remember Your Password

  1. On the login screen, select "Forgot Password?"

  2. Enter your email address associated with your account.

  3. Check your email for a password reset link.

  4. Click the link and create a new password.

  5. Try signing in with your new password.

Incorrect Email or Password Error

If you see "Incorrect email or password":

  1. Verify you're entering the correct email address.

  2. Check that Caps Lock is not on.

  3. Ensure you're typing your password correctly.

  4. If you're unsure of your password, use "Forgot Password?"

  5. Try signing in again.

Account Locked

If you see "Account Locked":

1. This may happen after multiple incorrect login attempts.

2. Wait 15-30 minutes before trying to sign in again.

3. Try signing in with correct credentials.

4. If the issue persists, contact SmartTV Support.

Email Verification Required

If your account requires email verification:

  1. Check your email inbox for a verification message.

  2. Click the verification link in the email.

  3. If you don't see the email, check your spam folder.

  4. Request a new verification email if needed.

  5. Try signing in again.

Account Suspended

If your account has been suspended:

  1. Check your email for notifications about the suspension.

  2. Contact SmartTV Support to restore your account.

  3. You may need to verify identity or update payment information.

Login on New Device

For security, you may need to verify login on a new device:

  1. Sign in with your credentials.

  2. Check your registered email for a verification request.

  3. Click the verification link.

  4. Complete any additional verification steps.

  5. You'll now have access on the new device.

Connection Issues

If you can't reach the login screen:

  1. Check your internet connection.

  2. Restart your device and try again.

  3. Try on a different network (mobile data vs Wi-Fi).

  4. Update your SmartTV app to the latest version.

  5. Clear app cache and try signing in again.

Multiple Account Issue

If you have multiple accounts:

  1. Make sure you're signing in with the correct email.

  2. Verify which email is associated with your account.

  3. If you can't remember, use "Forgot Password?" with each email.

Contact Support

If none of these steps work:

  1. Visit SmartTV Support website.

  2. Select Contact Us or Help.

  3. Describe your login issue.

  4. Provide your email address and account details.

  5. SmartTV support will assist you within 24 hours.

Prevention Tips

To avoid login issues:

  • Save your password in a secure password manager

  • Keep your email address current in account settings

  • Enable two-factor authentication if available

  • Log out securely when using shared devices
