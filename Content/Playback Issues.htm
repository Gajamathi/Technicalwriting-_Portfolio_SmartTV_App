Troubleshooting Playback Issues

If you're experiencing problems playing programs in SmartTV, follow these troubleshooting steps.

Video is not Pllaying

If a program won't start:

  1. Check your internet connection (minimum 5 Mbps for HD).

  2. Exit the app completely and reopen it.

  3. Try starting the program from the beginning.

  4. Restart your device and try again.

  5. Update SmartTV to the latest version.

Video Buffering or Stuttering

If your video keeps stopping to load:

  1. Check your internet speed (use a speed test app).

  2. Move closer to your Wi-Fi router.

  3. Reduce video quality in playback settings.

  4. Close other apps that use your internet.

  5. Restart your device.

  6. Switch to a wired connection if possible.

Video Quality Issues

If the video looks blurry or pixelated:

  1. During playback, select the quality settings icon.

  2. Check your current video quality.

  3. Try lowering the quality temporarily to test.

  4. If low quality plays fine, your connection may be weak.

  5. Increase quality when your internet improves.

No Audio or Sound Problems

If you can't hear the program:

  1. Check that device volume is not muted.

  2. Increase device volume using your device controls.

  3. In the app, check audio settings during playback.

  4. Try a different program to verify audio works.

  5. Restart the app if audio is completely missing.

Subtitles Not Showing

If subtitles aren't displaying:

  1. During playback, select the subtitle/CC icon.

  2. Ensure subtitles are enabled (toggle on).

  3. Select a subtitle language if options appear.

  4. Check if the program supports subtitles.

  5. Try selecting a different subtitle track if available.

Screen Goes Black

If the screen goes dark but you hear audio:

  1. The video may be loading. Wait a few seconds.

  2. Try pausing and resuming playback.

  3. Adjust brightness on your device.

  4. Exit and restart the program.

  5. If persistent, restart your device.

App Crashes During Playback

If SmartTV closes unexpectedly:

  1. Update SmartTV to the latest version.

  2. Clear the app cache (Settings → Apps → SmartTV → Clear Cache).

  3. Uninstall and reinstall SmartTV.

  4. Ensure your device has enough storage space.

  5. If issue persists, try on a different device.

Program Unavailable

If you see "Program Unavailable" message:

  1. The program may have ended or been removed.

  2. Check if you're subscribed to the required channel.

  3. Verify your subscription is active in Account Settings.

  4. Try accessing the program later.

  5. Contact Support if the issue continues.

Playback Freezing

If video freezes during playback:

  1. Pause and wait 10-15 seconds.

  2. Press Resume to continue.

  3. If it freezes frequently, lower video quality.

  4. Restart your app and try again.

  5. Check internet connection stability.

Subtitle and Audio Sync Issues

If subtitles don't match the audio:

  1. This may be a temporary streaming issue.

  2. Pause for 30 seconds and resume.

  3. Try a lower video quality.

  4. Restart the program from the beginning.

  5. Switch to a different subtitle language if available.

Different Device Issues

On TV/Smart TV:

- Ensure TV has latest software update

- Verify HDMI cable connection

- Try restarting the TV

On Mobile/Tablet:

- Check network connection strength

- Clear app cache

- Restart your device

On Computer:

- Update your web browser

- Disable browser extensions

- Use a supported browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)

Internet Connection Tips

To improve streaming:

  1. Connect to 5 GHz Wi-Fi (faster than 2.4 GHz).

  2. Use a wired Ethernet connection for best performance.

  3. Move away from objects blocking your Wi-Fi.

  4. Reduce the number of connected devices.

  5. Contact your internet provider if speeds are consistently low.

Check Device Compatibility

Playback may not work on incompatible devices:

  1. Verify your device meets SmartTV requirements.

  2. Check supported operating systems and versions.

  3. Update your device's operating system.

  4. Ensure device storage has at least 500 MB free space.

Contact Support

If playback issues persist:

  1. Note the program name and specific error message.

  2. Document when the issue occurs.

  3. Visit SmartTV Support website.

  4. Provide your device type and app version.

  5. Support will help diagnose and resolve the issue.

Prevention Tips

To avoid playback problems:

- Keep SmartTV updated to the latest version

- Maintain a strong internet connection

- Regularly clear app cache

- Update your device's operating system

- Restart your app occasionally
