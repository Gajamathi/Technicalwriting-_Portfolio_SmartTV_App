Understanding the Home Dashboard

Once you're signed in, you'll see your personalized Home dashboard.

Dashboard Layout

The SmartTV home dashboard displays:

  • Welcome Message - Personalized greeting with current date and time

  • Channels Tracked - Number of TV channels you're following

  • Favorites - Count of programs you've saved to favorites

  • Active Reminders - Number of active reminders set

  • This Week - Number of programs scheduled for this week

Your Personalized Schedule

The dashboard shows programs scheduled for this week from channels you track.

Each program displays:

  • Program thumbnail image

  • Program title

  • Category (Drama, Documentary, etc.)

  • Air time and channel information

Your Favorites

Below your schedule, you'll see saved programs from your Favorites list.You can quickly add new favorites from this section.

Navigation

Use the sidebar to navigate to different features:

  • Home - Return to your dashboard

  • Scheduling Your Programs - View your full program schedule

  • Managing Your Favorite Programs - Manage your saved programs

  • Setting Reminders for Programs - View and manage your reminders

  • Settings and Preferences - Customize your preferences
